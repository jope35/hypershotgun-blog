# Length and structure of a 4-minute post

## Reading speed (fetched, as of 2026-07-23 verification on page)
- Non-fiction silent reading: 238 wpm (Brysbaert 2019 meta-analysis, 190 studies, J. Memory and Language 109, 104047). Typical range 175-300 wpm.
- Dense technical material "commonly falls below 200 wpm" - page gives no source for this.
- Source: https://wordcount.com/guides/average-reading-speed ("What is the average reading speed?")

## Search-snippet only (not fetched, vendor pages)
- Medium default 265 wpm; other tools 200 wpm; technical 100-175 wpm. Sources disagree; treat loosely.
- Search: https://britetodo.com/tools/en/reading-time-calculator and others.

## Arithmetic (mine)
4 min x 200 = 800 words; x 238 = ~950; code/tables slow readers, so aim 700-850 words of prose.

## Structure (search results only, practitioner guides, weak)
- Open with a concrete number or sharp claim, not "In this blog post, I will...".
- Headings as signposts; 3-5 takeaway recap then a CTA.
- One template: 150-200 word intro and conclusion (for 1,000-1,200 word post).
- Sources: https://dev.to/hanzla-baig/essential-writing-tips-for-devto-community-f2e ; https://www.mejba.me/prompts/technical-blog-post-outline-1500-words (not opened)
