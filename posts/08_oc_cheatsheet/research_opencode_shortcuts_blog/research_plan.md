## Main question
How should I structure and write a ~4-minute intro blog post about OpenCode shortcuts (based on my cheatsheet) and a matching LinkedIn blurb?

## Subtopics (in order)
1. **Length and structure of a 4-minute blog post**
   - Evidence: words-per-minute reading speed for web/technical text; target word count; recommended structure for short intro/cheatsheet posts (hook, scannable sections, CTA).
2. **Writing about keyboard shortcuts / cheatsheet posts**
   - Evidence: how good shortcut/cheatsheet posts are organised (grouping, "top N first", tables vs prose, kbd formatting), developer-audience writing advice.
3. **LinkedIn blurb for a blog post**
   - Evidence: current best-practice length, hook placement ("see more" cutoff), link handling, hashtags, tone for developer posts.

## Combine
